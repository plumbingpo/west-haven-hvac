AC Repair in West Haven

Furnace Repair in West Haven

When the furnace clicks but never lights, lights and shuts off, or runs without warming the house, the cause is usually one component. We test the ignition, airflow, and safety controls to find it.   • No ignition when the heat calls
  • Burners light, then shut off within seconds
  • Cold air from the vents in heating mode
  • New noises when the furnace kicks on

If the compressor or heat exchanger has failed, the unit is past 12 to 15 years old, or repairs are stacking up season after season, replacement is the smarter investment. Our technicians walk you through both numbers honestly so you can decide with real information.
