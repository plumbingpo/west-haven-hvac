Upfront Pricing

Clear Pricing Before Any Work Starts

Before we start any repair, we figure out exactly what's wrong. Our technicians check your system's age and tonnage, the refrigerant charge, the heat exchanger and coils, and the condition of your ductwork. After that you get an honest recommendation and the full price up front, whether the job is swapping a worn capacitor or replacing the whole system. No hidden fees, no surprise add-ons.

The trouble with HVAC failures is that many of them develop out of sight. A pinhole leak in the evaporator coil slowly drains refrigerant, the blower bearings wear down gradually, and a weak capacitor lasts right up until the first hot afternoon, when the compressor won't start. When bills climb, airflow drops, or ice forms on the lines, have the system checked before a repair turns into a replacement.

Do you know how old your system is? Once an air conditioner or furnace passes the 12-year mark, it's worth planning the next one before the current one forces the issue. Emergency replacements happen on the worst days, in heat waves and cold snaps, when choices are limited and everyone is uncomfortable. Our technicians know how the West Haven climate affects equipment and will tell you plainly whether yours has years left or is nearing the end.

Maintenance Plans

Maintenance Plans for West Haven Homes

Each maintenance visit from West Haven HVAC is far more than a quick filter swap. Our technician cleans the coils, verifies the refrigerant charge, tests capacitors and contactors, inspects the burners and heat exchanger, clears the condensate drain, replaces the filter, and measures static pressure across the ducts. Minor faults get caught while they're inexpensive to correct, and your system keeps running at full efficiency.

We offer flexible maintenance options for homeowners, with spring cooling tune-ups and fall heating tune-ups for most systems. That's especially important in dusty areas, where dirty condenser coils choke heat rejection and drive up energy use. Each home is assessed individually, and our technicians tailor the plan to your equipment's age, your household's comfort needs, and your local climate.

Think of maintenance as protection for equipment you've already paid for. Each tune-up keeps the coils clean, the charge correct, and the filter fresh, which lowers monthly energy bills while easing the strain on the compressor and blower. For most households, the plan costs far less than an early replacement.

Right-Sized Systems

Comfort Sized to Your Home's Demand

When it comes to HVAC, bigger is not better. An oversized unit short-cycles, wastes energy, and never runs long enough to pull humidity out of the air, while an undersized one runs nonstop and still can't keep up on the hottest days. That's why we never just copy the tonnage of the old unit. Instead, we run a proper load calculation using your square footage, insulation, windows, and ductwork, and for mini-splits we size each head to the room it serves.

Picking a new system comes down to more than a SEER2 number. Our technician will compare heat pumps against a furnace-and-AC pair, and ducted against ductless, walking through operating costs, electrical and venting requirements, and the installation itself. Families with a growing household frequently do best with two-stage or variable-speed equipment, which holds more even temperatures from room to room and uses less energy. You choose what fits your home and budget.

Common Problems

Heating and Cooling Problems We Solve in West Haven

No cooling, a furnace that refuses to light, a frozen evaporator coil, a slow refrigerant leak: each of these problems has its own cause and calls for a different repair. West Haven HVAC tracks down the exact fault before swapping any parts, whether it's a condenser that hums but won't start, a short-cycling furnace, a thermostat that keeps losing its program, a clogged condensate drain, or a mini-split throwing error codes.

Some problems are mostly a nuisance, while others pose real risks. A weak capacitor simply keeps the compressor from starting, but a cracked heat exchanger can leak combustion gases into your living space, and a blocked condensate drain can quietly ruin a ceiling. We match the fix to the failure: capacitors, contactors, and fan motors on cooling systems, igniters, flame sensors, and control boards on furnaces, sealed and repaired ductwork where conditioned air is bleeding into the attic, and a proper refrigerant recharge after a repaired leak. Whatever you're facing, our team has the parts and experience to handle it.
